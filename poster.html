Universal design in practice

Designed for clarity and control

  • Perceivable: high-contrast text, meaningful labels, descriptive image alternative text and visible focus states.
  • Operable: keyboard-friendly links and forms, large touch targets, simple navigation and no time-limited actions.
  • Understandable: consistent buttons, familiar shopping language, inline validation and clear feedback after actions.
  • Robust: semantic HTML, labelled form controls, live status messages and responsive layouts for different screens.
