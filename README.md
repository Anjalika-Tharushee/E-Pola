# E-Pola

E-Pola is a customer shopping experience inspired by the traditional Sri Lankan marketplace "Pola".

## Run locally

Open `index.html` directly in Chrome. No XAMPP, server, database, or package installation is required. The shop, product details, cart, wishlist, registration, and sign-in screens are views within this page so they share the same browser storage when opened as a local file.

## Customer shopping features

- Browse featured devices and product categories.
- Read about E-Pola and contact the team through the listed email address.
- Search devices, filter by category and maximum price, and sort the catalog.
- Open product details and add devices to the cart or wishlist.
- Change cart quantities and remove saved items.
- Check out with delivery details and cash on delivery; demo orders are saved in this browser.
- Create a demo customer account and sign in.

Cart, wishlist, demo orders, and demo account data are stored in the browser's `localStorage`. Clearing browser data removes them. Product descriptions and prices are sample data. Checkout does not process payments or send order notifications. Registration and sign-in are client-side demonstrations only; do not use real passwords or personal information.
